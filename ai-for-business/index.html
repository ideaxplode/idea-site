AI could help by

Reading the incoming information, picking out what matters, and preparing the next step.

  • Read forms, emails or documents
  • Extract and organise the useful details
  • Update the right record or prepare a task
  • Send unclear cases to a person
Good first move

Choose one repetitive task with clear rules and enough volume to make the saved time visible.

AI could help by

Watching one enquiry channel, preparing a relevant response, and making sure the next action has an owner.

  • Recognise what the enquiry is about
  • Prepare a reply for review
  • Remind the right person to follow up
  • Keep a record of what happened
Good first move

Start with the channel where the most valuable enquiries are currently delayed or lost.

AI could help by

Finding approved information quickly and preparing a clear answer, while your team handles situations that need judgment.

  • Search the information you trust
  • Draft a consistent answer
  • Show where the answer came from
  • Escalate unusual or sensitive questions
Good first move

Begin with a narrow set of common questions and keep a person approving every answer until it proves reliable.

AI could help by

Bringing scattered information into one useful summary, so people spend less time hunting and asking around.

  • Collect information from existing tools
  • Summarise what changed
  • Highlight missing or conflicting details
  • Prepare the brief your team already needs
Good first move

Pick one report, meeting or handoff that repeatedly sends people searching for the same information.

AI could help by

Turning current information into a timely picture of what needs attention, without taking the decision away from you.

  • Prepare regular business summaries
  • Flag unusual changes or exceptions
  • Explain the information in plain language
  • Suggest questions worth asking next
Good first move

Choose one recurring decision and define what you wish you knew before making it.
